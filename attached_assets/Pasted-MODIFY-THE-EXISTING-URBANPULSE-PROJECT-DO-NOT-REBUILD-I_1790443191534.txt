==================================================

UrbanPulse is a unified AI-powered urban intelligence platform for Mumbai.

Core concept:

PUBLIC BUSES
→ act as mobile urban sensing units

EDGE AI
→ processes camera information locally

CITY INTELLIGENCE
→ aggregates fleet-wide observations

CITIZENS
→ can report and validate urban issues

MULTIPLE BUSES
→ can provide multi-perspective incident evidence

REPAIR VERIFICATION
→ checks whether reported problems were actually fixed

PRIVACY + FEDERATED LEARNING

18. FINAL PRODUCT STORY
==================================================

The final demo should communicate this story:

1. Buses continuously sense the city.

2. Edge AI detects road, traffic, infrastructure and safety conditions.

3. Citizens can also report problems.

4. Citizen reports are automatically matched with existing issues instead of creating duplicate complaints.

5. Multiple buses can provide independent observations and reconstruct incidents.

6. When authorities repair an issue, buses can re-observe the location and help verify whether the problem was actually fixed.

7. Authorities can ask the city questions naturally through Ask the City.

8. Voice and local languages are simply additional ways to interact with Ask the City.

9. Federated Learning allows simulated collaborative model improvement while raw training data remains local.

10. Privacy processing protects sensitive visual data.
